## Audio & Voice Mastering Summary
- **Voice processing chain**:
  1. `highpass=f=80`: Eliminates sub-rumble and proximity boom.
  2. `equalizer=f=250:t=q:w=1.2:g=-1.5`: Dips muddy mid-bass frequencies for speech intelligibility.
  3. `equalizer=f=3000:t=q:w=1.5:g=2.0`: Adds crisp vocal presence and consonant articulation.
